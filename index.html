Molineux Mix Reader

Ad-free, clean view of Molineux Mix on your iPhone, with a Live button for comms threads.

  1. Tap the Share button below and choose Add Bookmark. Name it MM and tap Save. (It saves this page; we'll swap the address next.)
  2. Tap the book icon (Bookmarks), then Edit, and tap the MM bookmark.
  3. Tap the address line under the name, clear it (the ⓧ), then Paste. Tap Done.

Using it

Open any Molineux Mix page, tap the address bar, type MM and tap the bookmark when it appears.

On a thread, tap Live to jump to the newest post and get new posts every 20 seconds. Tap ‹ for the thread list.
